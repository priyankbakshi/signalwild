01 / The unmet need

You know your pet.
What happens when someone else takes over?

Experienced owners understand many familiar signals. But that knowledge lives inside a relationship. It is hard to transfer, incomplete when nobody is watching, and often unavailable to the people caring for an unfamiliar animal.

01 , THE HANDOVER GAP

Understanding disappears at the handover.

Adoption, boarding, shelters, sitters and veterinarians must learn the same individual animal's routines and signals again.

02 , THE OBSERVATION GAP

Important behaviour goes unseen.

What happens when owners leave? Which patterns change slowly? Passive observation could reveal events that familiarity alone cannot.

03 , THE EVIDENCE GAP

Verified understanding beats captions.

The next frontier links animal signals to observable outcomes and meaningful human responses.

02 / The new premise

Let animals communicate naturally.

SignalWild adapts to each animal through natural vocalisations, gestures, movement, context and repeated interactions.

04 / The core invention

Understand. Verify. Respond.

Instead of offering a plausible translation, SignalWild would test hypotheses against observable outcomes and help humans use familiar, non-aversive communication cues that the particular animal already recognises.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Learn natural signals.

Vocalisations, movement, posture, environmental context, existing routines and individual history. Physiological data can be tested later where reliable and comfortable.

02 / VERIFY

Prove useful understanding.

Compare predictions with unseen behaviour, alternative explanations, existing owner knowledge and conventional monitoring. Report uncertainty when evidence is weak.

03 / RESPOND

Close the loop.

Help authorised people respond using a cue or action already familiar to the animal; measure the animal's voluntary response without shock or coercive training.

↻ Each verified interaction is potential training evidence for the individual model and broader species priors.
05 / The technology

From raw signals to an individual communication model.

Our technical stack combines pretrained audio and visual encoders, temporal event alignment, species priors and an individual memory. Predictions carry linked observations, confidence and a response test.

01 / SENSEMultimodal inputs

Vocal patterns, animal pose, gaze, movement, environmental cues and optional physiological change.

02 / FUSEEvent timeline

Synchronised clips, sensor quality checks and sequences before, during and after an interaction.

03 / LEARNIndividual adaptation

A foundation representation conditioned on each animal's history and independently observed outcomes.

04 / ACTUnderstand + respond

Evidence-linked interpretation, uncertainty and familiar non-aversive human cues, with measurable feedback.

Research advantage: a longitudinal dataset that connects individual signals, surroundings, physiological context, human responses and the animal's subsequent actions.
06 / First product application

A Communication Passport for every animal.

The Communication Passport is the first application of our sensing and intelligence platform. It carries each animal's communication history, supporting observations and familiar human responses across care settings.

The commercially testable wedge

Let understanding travel with the animal.

Imagine adopting a rescue dog, handing it over to a sitter, or transferring care to a boarding facility. The passport would help unfamiliar caregivers recognise observable individual patterns, while retaining context and uncertainty.

First buyers to validate: shelters / adoption partners, boarding and care providers, and households at care transitions. Initial market: shelters, adoption partners, boarding and care providers.

07 / Scientific validation

Better than guessing.
Better than basic pet monitoring.

We measure how much personalised intelligence improves an unfamiliar caregiver's predictions, choices and responses, using previously unseen interactions.

The control conditions

What already exists

Compare an unfamiliar caregiver unaided, a caregiver using conventional care notes, a context-only predictor, and a generic species-level AI system.

SignalWild test condition

Personalised assistance

The same caregiver is assisted by an evidence-linked, individual model. We compare held-out behavioural predictions, voluntary responses, calibrated uncertainty and decision usefulness.

MEASURE 01

Predictive value

Can personalisation outperform contexts and generic models on new interactions?

MEASURE 02

Human usefulness

Do caregivers make more appropriate, independently assessed decisions?

MEASURE 03

Generalisation

Do results replicate across animals, households and different caregivers?

09 / Development roadmap

An engineering plan with measurable milestones.

Software first using existing smartphones and cameras. Custom collars or glasses integrations only when their incremental value is established.

0-30 DAYS

Prototype + protocol

Operational recording and annotation tools, defined behaviours, adviser outreach and a pre-registered evaluation design.

MONTHS 2-3

Animal cohort

Recruit a small consented pilot (target 20-30 dogs) with longitudinal observations and caregiver involvement.

MONTHS 4-6

Personalisation test

Run held-out comparisons against notes, context and generic AI; publish reproducible comparisons and findings.

MONTHS 7-12

Commercial proof

Test adoption/care partnerships, pricing, retention and whether a specialised sensor materially changes performance.
